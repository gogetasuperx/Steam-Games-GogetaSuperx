About This Game

Deep within the Webbing Waves, lost souls have merged into one. Persistence, and a stubborn will, seem to be the only way out.

Explore. Equip. Fight.

Dash'n'Drops fuses the gameplay of Platform-Fighters (Super Smash Bros., Rivals of Aether...) with the unpredictable progression of a modern Roguelite (Dead Cells, Hades...). Taking place during the events of Decline's Drops, dive into the network's strata to plunder power, stack up Power, Speed, Defense, and Jump, and come out strong enough to bring down the entities locking the system.

Improve. Adapt. Update.

Build your own arsenal in a race against the clock before the final showdown.

Complete. Chain. Discover.

Countless Modes and surprises await to be unlocked! Variety is the spice of life, even when you're trapped in a virtual world.

Features:

  • Rediscover the feel of a competitive fighting game in a solo adventure where every hit counts. Send your enemies flying or drain their health bar with surgical precision.

  • Fully customize your moveset in real time. Find and equip a wide range of Diskettes to shape your Smash attacks, Tilts, aerials, and more. Every run offers a brand-new combat identity.

  • Optimize your core stats (Speed, Jump, Power, Defense) and collect passive items to become an anomaly the system can no longer contain.

  • Put your build to the test against the Eternals, massive bosses corrupted to the core, designed to push your technical skill and strategy to their limits.

  • A fully hand-drawn and hand-animated world, crafted and imagined with care, homemade!
