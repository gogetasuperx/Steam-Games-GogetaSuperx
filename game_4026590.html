About This Game


In Nocthra, you play as a Necromancer accidentally awakened by villagers who shattered an ancient seal.
Defend yourself from the living, raise the dead, and strategically command chaos in a game infused with roguelike elements.


Corpses are the Necromancer’s greatest resource. Summon minions from fallen bodies or convert them into dark energy to fuel your rituals.



Gather Dark Energy and unlock unique Sigils that empower your minions, alter the battlefield, and grant the Necromancer new abilities.
