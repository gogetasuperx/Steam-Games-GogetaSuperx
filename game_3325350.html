Official Description

About This Game

In Catfall, you are the most precious member of a spaceship crew — a cat whose warmth and gentle purrs bring comfort to everyone on board. Life is perfect… until a sudden disaster throws everything into chaos.

CHOOSE YOUR CAT

Of course, you can choose which one you want to play: an orange troublemaker, a calm grey homebody, a white handsome dust collector, or this black ball of fur with green eyes!

SOLVE PUZZLES

For a cat, you’re surprisingly good at solving various puzzles. Push a button here, drag a cable there, and suddenly — the power’s back on! Maybe it’s just feline curiosity at work, but does it matter if it works.

BE A CAT AND HAVE FUN

Don’t forget to be your adorable feline self — hunt for snacks, claim the coziest nap spots, and keep your spirits high. After all, your well-being might just be the key to surviving this journey. And remember: naughtiness is always an option.

OVERCOME DANGERS AND SURVIVE

Fun is fine, but don’t forget the disaster. A kitty probably can’t repair a high-tech starship, but you’ll still be invaluable to your crew. Use your agility and quiet sneaking to help the mission. Most importantly — keep track of how many lives you have left, and don’t forget to land on your paws!
