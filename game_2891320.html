About This Game

City-building meets deck-building in Chaos Islands. Defend against demons, explore the skies!

Establish and expand your floating settlement in the skies, strategically placing structures to gather essential resources. Raise defenses to protect against demon attacks and ensure the survival of your community.

Defend your settlement with diverse strategies and card abilities to repel demon assaults and keep your settlement safe.

Customize your arsenal by collecting cards that represent various constructions and spells empowering you to adapt and overcome the challenges of the skies.

Navigate through dynamic events that shape the fate of your settlement, presenting both opportunities and challenges as you strive to survive in the skies.
