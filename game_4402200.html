About This Game

BASE BUILDING

Transform a barren planet into a lush world habitable for humans. Gather resources, build and expand your base, deploy terraforming machines—and defend them from an ever-present alien threat.

DEFEND

Terraforming attracts attention. As alien forces grow hostile, they send waves of attackers to dismantle your base and machines. Construct defensive structures, upgrade your arsenal, and repel each assault.

SURVIVAL

Resources are limited, and survival is never guaranteed. Explore the planet to gather materials and locate hidden caches of food, water, and seeds. Grow crops, construct water purification systems, and keep your colony alive.
