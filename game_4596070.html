About This Game

Explore the endless dungeon of a dead god. Roll, manipulate, and weaponize your dice - and if nothing else works, just throw one straight at the enemy like an old-fashioned fireball. Dice Crawler is built around a unique dice-based combat system that blends TTRPG and CRPG elements into one experience.

Potions, weapons, armor, and even monster guts - as you crawl through the dungeon, you’ll find all kinds of gear to use or sell for profit to your friendly dwarf merchant (for a “fair” price). There may also be a chest with a dice lock!

The road to the dead god’s lair is crawling with all sorts of critters and monsters. Kill them with skill, guts, and a sprinkle of luck. The deeper you go, the more wicked and powerful the monsters become - but so do you!

This is a small game made by a solo developer in my free time. I’ll do my best to respond to and fix issues as quickly as I can, but time (and space) has its limits! Either way, feel free to reach out with any feedback or suggestions, and most importantly, have a great time playing.

Cheers!
